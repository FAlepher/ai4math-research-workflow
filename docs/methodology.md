# Methodology and knowledge continuity

The workflow is human-directed: a researcher determines priorities, evaluates significance and decides when to continue, merge or abandon a line of inquiry. AI sessions can take on research, integration, adversarial review and experimentation. Two further roles have distinct purposes:

* **Understanding:** receives forwarded outputs from research, integration or coordination and explains them to the human researcher, helping the human understand and evaluate the work. The separate role is intended to preserve the effectiveness of each function; it is not an automatic guarantee of independence.
* **Archaeology:** examines historical research materials, excavates and reconstructs VWL records, and develops a compressed understanding of the global research history. This supports continuity and helps avoid repeated exploration of known dead ends.

These are functional roles; one model or shared assumptions do not automatically provide independent verification. The description concerns the research practice and does not imply that the browser extension automates these roles.

## Flexible use under limited allowances

The researcher developed this workflow for sustained GPT-assisted research with limited usage allowances. The choice of roles and handoffs is flexible rather than a fixed sequence repeated every round; it can change with the needs of the research.

The researcher describes a distinction in how context is carried:

* Lightweight tasks can be handled directly in Chat.
* The **coordinator** and **archaeology** roles use context and handoff prompts to carry a broad view across conversations.
* **Research, integration and understanding** are active modules that can be opened as needed. When a task is clearly stated, they can work from the task, relevant project memory, Wealth records and directional prompts supplied as needed, rather than always carrying the entire research history.

This is a practical design principle, not a claim that these roles need no context or that any particular prompt is sufficient for every proof or explanation. Preserving useful knowledge and failed paths is intended to reduce repeated work, but this project does not measure or claim a particular efficiency improvement.

Research and integration are complementary. Exploration proposes paths and conjectures. As confirmed by the researcher, integration attempts to reconstruct a complete proof from its assumptions, identifies missing implications and checks that local results can be combined on the same object. Adversarial review searches for counterexamples and missing hypotheses. Experimental checks and formal proof have different scopes and should be labeled accordingly.

## VWL: two axes

* Chronological archives preserve discovery order, provenance and the qualification of a result at the time. Later corrections should not rewrite earlier states silently.
* **View** collects valuable perspectives and research directions; it does not imply a theorem.
* **Wealth** collects positive mathematical assets with assumptions, conclusion, dependencies, source and qualification.
* **Law / No-Go** preserves false conjectures, counterexamples, scope and failure mechanisms in enough detail to avoid repeating dead ends.

Positive knowledge can be compressed as redundant versions are reconciled; negative knowledge often needs more detail to preserve why a route failed. Artifact records can support continuity across sessions, but a record or a passing computation does not by itself establish a general mathematical proof.

This description draws on the researcher's account, an AI-generated archaeology summary and two private sample documents supplied by the researcher. The samples show a chronological research freeze and a cross-time result index with status categories, source references, scope and corrections. Their underlying source reports and mathematical proofs have not been independently checked. The samples remain private, and no specific mathematical result or provenance is asserted here.

## Prompt templates and practice

The private project contains role and handoff prompt templates that ask for traceable claims, explicit uncertainty, failed attempts and adversarial review. These templates describe intended behavior; their proposed machine-readable handoffs and automated ledger updates are not evidence that those mechanisms were implemented. One template restricts integration to editing and records of gaps, which differs from the researcher's confirmed description above. The reason for that difference has not been established.
