# Synthetic research handoff

This invented example illustrates the documentation format, not a historical result, a verified research archive, or an automated exchange.

## Coordinator → research

**Question:** Is every positive integer greater than one a prime number?

**Deliverable:** Either a proof or a counterexample; state the scope and check the example. This is a lightweight synthetic task; no project-specific Wealth or historical source is assumed.

## Research → integration / audit

**Proposed finding:** The statement is false. The integer 4 is greater than one and is divisible by 2, so it is not prime.

**Check:** `4 = 2 × 2`, and 2 is neither 1 nor 4.

## Audit → coordinator

**Verdict:** The counterexample refutes the universal statement. It does not imply that all integers greater than one are composite; 2 is prime.

**Open question:** Which additional conditions are sufficient for primality? This example does not answer that question.

## Illustrative VWL records

* **V — View:** Test a universal statement on small examples before seeking a general proof.
* **L — No-Go:** “Every integer greater than one is prime” fails at 4. The failure applies to the universal claim, not to individual primality claims.

No W entry is automatically created from this illustrative exchange. A real record would need its own source, scope, proof or verification status, and the qualification appropriate to the claim.
