# Example: a research perspective and a failed claim

**This is an invented, elementary example**, written to show the structure of a record. It is not taken from the researcher's private VWL archives, and it is not a claim about a research breakthrough.

## Task and audit

* **Question:** Is every positive integer greater than one prime?
* **Proposed universal claim:** Every integer greater than one is prime.
* **Counterexample:** `4 = 2 × 2`; both factors are positive integers other than 1 and 4, so 4 is not prime.
* **Status:** The universal claim is refuted by the displayed counterexample. This says nothing about whether other individual integers are prime.

## V — View

When examining a universal statement, try small cases before investing in a general proof. This is a research perspective, not a theorem about all proof strategies.

## L — Law / No-Go

* **Rejected statement:** Every positive integer greater than one is prime.
* **Why it fails:** Composite integers exist; 4 is a concrete example.
* **Scope:** This example rejects the universal statement only. It does not imply that every integer greater than one is composite.
* **Source and verification:** This synthetic record and the elementary factorization above; no external research source or automated checker was used.

## W — Wealth

No Wealth entry is created by this example. In actual work, a positive result needs its precise assumptions, conclusion, source, dependencies and appropriate proof qualification. A record alone does not certify the proof.
